Every piece is its family's silhouette in the seat color with an ink outline, legible at 28–36 px (UXDEC-018).

`AOH.pieceArt(kind, seatColor, px, count, signature)`. Explorer (compass rose), Pioneer (house and founding flag) and Envoy (sealed letter) have their own shapes, never a shared disc; discs are kept for inhabitants inside a city. A stack shows its leading family and the count; civil pieces stand beside an army. A signature family carries the star.
