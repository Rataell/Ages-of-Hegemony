A true top-down city that fills its hex; its level (1 Posto … 7 Metrópole Imperial) reads through the urban plan, never through height (UXDEC-016).

Draw with `AOH.cityArt(level, 50, {color: seatColor, seed})` inside the hex group. The civic roof and the hex rim take the seat color; everything else is neutral. `depth: true` adds light shadows and belongs to the Focus/Inspector only. Far zoom uses `AOH.citySeal(level, r, color)`.
