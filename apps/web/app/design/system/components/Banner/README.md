The civilization's small standard: field in the seat color, emblem in ink, shape by civilization.

Six shapes (reta, bifurcada, flâmula, gonfalão, escudo, arredondada) times three seals make the eighteen civilizations unique without relying on color. Fly it at the city's corner, beside a selected piece, in the lobby, diplomacy, ranking and on capture (the old banner tilts and fades). Draw with `AOH.bannerArt(mark, seatColor, px)`.
