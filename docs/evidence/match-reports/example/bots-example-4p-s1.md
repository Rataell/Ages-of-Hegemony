## O que este relatório não sabe
- **UNAVAILABLE** · match.finishedAt: The final state carries no clock and the caller gave no date.
- **CALCULATED** · result.prestige: milestones (3 each), Ambições, Obras (4 each, without civilization bonuses) and Regiões (1 each) are derived from the final state; `other` is the remainder (cards, Ressonância, Ameaças, treaty penalties) and can be negative.
- **UNAVAILABLE** · civilizations.cities.elevations: No per-civilization counter of the Elevar action exists; see byLevel and firstRoundAtLevel.
- **PARTIAL** · civilizations.government: Only the final government and the Era of the last reform are preserved (earlier governments are not).
- **PARTIAL** · civilizations.research.order: The acquisition order is preserved; the round of each advancement is not (the cumulative count per round is in the round log).
- **CALCULATED** · civilizations.cities.atStart: Cities the seat originally owned that were already founded in round 1 (initial cities), whoever holds them now.
- **CALCULATED** · civilizations.research.heritages: Researched ids with the heritage prefix (her-).
- **PARTIAL** · economy.ledger: Flows come from the legacy five-pool ledger: wood, stone and metal are merged in `materials`, Cultura has no flows. Era-turn effects (Pulso, rewards) fall outside the round ledger.
- **UNAVAILABLE** · economy.flows8: The engine does not record per-resource flows for wood, stone, metal and Cultura; only instrumented simulation runs carry them.
- **PARTIAL** · economy.globalProduction: Eight keys but all civilizations summed and only the PRODUCE action output.
- **CALCULATED** · economy.shortage: Rounds with FOOD_SHORTAGE / UPKEEP_SHORTAGE / ADMINISTRATIVE_OVEREXTENSION events addressed to the seat.
- **CALCULATED** · actions.ordersUnused: available (rounds × Orders per round) minus committed; `committed` counts Orders committed, not Orders that succeeded.
- **PARTIAL** · actions.failedActions: Counts only actions the engine rejected at resolution (ACTION_FAILED / ORDER_FAILED); commands refused when sent are not in the state.
- **PARTIAL** · conflicts.warsDeclared: Only the number of declarations (public events). Who declared on whom is not recorded without the display names, and `state.wars` holds only the wars still active.
- **PARTIAL** · conflicts.threats: Threat counters are global; destroyed Acampamentos leave no per-seat record except the world-battle list and THREAT events.
- **CALCULATED** · conflicts.perSeat.unitsLost: Losses are assigned to a side by membership of the unit ids in the battle record.
- **CALCULATED** · timeline: Curated milestones from structured records (Era changes, foundations, captures, levels, Obras, milestones, Hegemony, decisive battles); not a log of operations. Text is generated, never copied from private events.
